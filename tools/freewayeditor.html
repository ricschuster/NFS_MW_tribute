Freeway Loop

What this is

Kestrel Bay's interstate loop, today an authored path instead of a computed rectangle (#261). Drag it into the hills: leave the built city, go round, come back. The relief and grade shading are the same ground the generator drives it against.

Drag a point to move it. Click on the loop between two points to add one there. Select a point and press Delete to remove it.
