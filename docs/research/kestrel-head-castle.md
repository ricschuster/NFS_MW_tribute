# Kestrel Head: the castle, and what it was modelled on (#454)

The owner asked on 2026-09-30 for the fort on Kestrel Head to be modelled on
**Burgruine Schaunberg**, a castle ruin in Upper Austria
([Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgruine_Schaunberg)), and shared
a photograph of the plan board at the ruin. This is the note CLAUDE.md asks
for: the influence named where the reasoning lives, and nothing of it in what
ships. No name, no plan and no geometry of Schaunberg is in the game; what was
taken is how such a castle is put together.

## What was read from it

- **A long castle on a hill, about 17,500 m².** An outer bailey (*Vorburg*)
  strung out along the ridge, and the inner castle (*Kernburg*) at the high
  end. The old way in ran through the bailey, past its stables and granary.
- **Gates in a row.** A main gate, a gatekeeper's lodge and a powder tower by
  it, then the inner castle's gate. Ditches and drawbridges between the parts.
- **A ring wall about 4 m thick** round the inner castle, with towers on it.
- **The keep, 32 m, on a five-sided plan**, its point aimed at the direction an
  attack would come from.
- **Inside the inner castle** the hall (*Palas*, a Gothic doorway surviving),
  the chapel, living quarters and service rooms round the courtyard.

## How it maps onto Kestrel Head

The hill already had the shape: one road climbing the ridge from the north-west
and running 175 m to the summit (`k1`), and the summit itself.

- **The bailey** is walled along both sides of `k1`, 28 m out, from a main gate
  55 m up the road to the inner castle: wall towers, a powder tower at the
  front corner, and the gatekeeper's lodge, stables, granary and a smithy as
  roofless ruins. The main gate is 55 m up rather than at the junction because
  the road turns onto `k1` in a hairpin, and a gate 30 m after it put the
  Kestrel Climb's reference driver into the wall.
- **The inner castle** is an oval ring wall round the summit, with the inner
  gate where `k1` meets it and a back gate where `k2` leaves for the gravel
  track down (a second way out the original did not have, chosen by the owner).
  The keep stands just inside the inner gate, its point aimed down the
  approach, and carries the beacon: the owner asked for a fort *and signal
  station*, and the keep is both. The hall, chapel, living quarters and service
  rooms face the courtyard.
- **Together** about 17,000 m², as the original.

## Not done

- No ditches: they would be cuts in the terrain across the ridge, which the
  road would then need bridges over.
- The old tree by the original's gate is drawn with the game's one tree, a
  conifer.
